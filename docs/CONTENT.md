## German 101 — glossary (all required)
Every German word glossed in English at first use, with phonetic respelling where
it will be spoken aloud.
- Prost — cheers (PROHST)
- O'zapft is — "it's tapped" (oh-TSAHPFT iss)
- Maß — a one-litre mug of beer (MAHSS)
- Tracht — traditional festival dress (TRAHKT)
- Lederhosen — leather knee-breeches (LAY-der-hoh-zen)
- Dirndl — the dress-blouse-apron outfit (DEERN-dl) — include the apron-knot code
  (left = single, right = taken, front-centre = virgin/undecided, back = widowed or serving)
- Schuhplattler — the Bavarian shoe-slapping dance (SHOO-platt-ler)
- Prüfung — a test or trial (PROO-foong)
- Gemütlichkeit — warm cosy belonging (guh-MOOT-likh-kite)
- Ein Prosit — "a toast" (ine PROH-zit)
- Zicke zacke — means nothing. Genuinely nothing. (TSIK-uh TSAK-uh)
- Bierdeckel — beer mat / coaster (BEER-deck-el)
- Zapfhahn — the tap itself (TSAPF-hahn)
- Currywurst — (KUH-ree-voorst) bratwurst, sliced, drowned in curry ketchup.
  The caterer's whole thing.
